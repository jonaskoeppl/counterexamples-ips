# Symmetric exclusion on two half-spaces of $\mathbb{Z}^3$ — follow-up questions

1. Interacting exchange dynamics with rates bounded away from zero on **all** bonds of $\mathbb{Z}^d$, $d \ge 3$: are there non-reversible stationary measures (with finite energy)? — `open` (Remark 8.2(e) of the source)
2. Does the stationary measure $\mu$ of Theorem 8.3 (interacting dynamics on $G$) have finite energy? — `open` (Remark 8.4(c))
3. Same construction with a potential that does not decay at infinity (e.g. translation-invariant in each half-space): do non-reversible stationary measures with different chemical potentials at the two ends exist? — `open` (Remark 8.4(d))
4. For SEP on a connected graph of bounded degree: is there a non-reversible stationary measure **iff** simple random walk has a non-constant bounded harmonic function? "If": the construction above. "Only if": Liggett's characterisation. — `known` modulo checking that Liggett's characterisation applies on general graphs (to be written up)
5. How few bonds of $\mathbb{Z}^3$ must be switched off? Removing finitely many bonds should not create non-constant bounded harmonic functions. Which infinite sets of removed bonds do? — `open` (in this repository)
6. Spin-flip analogue: is there any mechanism, other than conservation, that sustains a current in a stationary state of a positive-rate spin system on $\mathbb{Z}^3$? This is the Holley–Stroock question for $d \ge 3$. — `open`
