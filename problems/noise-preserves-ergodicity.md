# Does adding noise preserve ergodicity?

**Status:** open (in this repository)

## Question

Let $c(x,\eta)$ be the rates of an ergodic spin system on $\{0,1\}^{\mathbb{Z}^d}$. For $\varepsilon > 0$, consider the system with rates $c(x,\eta) + \varepsilon$. Is it ergodic?

## Variants

- attractive vs. non-attractive rates
- symmetric noise vs. noise biased towards one spin value
- all $\varepsilon > 0$ vs. small or large $\varepsilon$
- exclusion-type or mixed dynamics; probabilistic cellular automata

## Related

- Liggett (1985), Ch. I.4 (ergodicity criteria, e.g. the $M < \varepsilon$ condition) and Ch. III (spin systems).
- P. Gács, Reliable cellular automata with self-organization, *J. Stat. Phys.* 103 (2001): non-ergodicity that survives all small noise (the reverse phenomenon).
