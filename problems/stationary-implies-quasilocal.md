# Are stationary measures of positive-rate systems quasilocal?

**Status:** open on $\mathbb{Z}^d$; false on the 3-regular tree (entry 0004)

## Question

Consider an interacting particle system with finite local state space and finite-range transition rates that are uniformly bounded away from zero. Show that if $\mu$ is a stationary measure for the IPS, then it is necessarily quasilocal.

## Motivation

Elliptic regularity: stationary measures are the solutions of $\mathscr{L}^*\mu = 0$. Positive rates play the role of uniform ellipticity, quasilocality the role of regularity.

## Variants

- $\mathbb{Z}^d$ vs. other graphs (trees, amenable graphs of exponential growth)
- spin systems (one site changes per jump) vs. block updates
- all stationary measures vs. extremal or translation-invariant ones
- two-letter vs. larger local state space

## Related

- R. Holley, *Comm. Math. Phys.* 23 (1971); B. Jahnel, C. Külske, *Comm. Math. Phys.* 366 (2019): relative-entropy methods on $\mathbb{Z}^d$.
- P. Gács (2001), A. L. Toom (1980): positive-rate systems with several stationary measures, whose mixtures are candidates on $\mathbb{Z}^d$.
